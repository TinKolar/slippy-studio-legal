Slippy Rails – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Slippy Rails mobile game (the "Game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is made by Tin Kolar, publishing as Slippy Studio ("we", "us"). It is free to play and supported by ads.

The short version: we have no accounts, no names, no email addresses and no servers of our own. Your progress is saved on your phone. Gameplay analytics and personalized ads are only turned on if you agree, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, coins, purchased upgrades, settings, lifetime game statistics, the age you entered and your privacy choice on your device only. We do not receive a copy. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data, deletes all of it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the third-party services below. They collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies.

Gameplay analytics – Google Firebase Analytics

Only with your consent. Analytics is switched off when the Game is installed and stays off until you answer the privacy question on first launch. If you agree, Firebase collects:

  • gameplay events, such as runs started and finished, score, events reached, abilities used, rewarded ads watched and in-game coins earned or spent;
  • a random app-instance identifier generated for your installation, and, where available, your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and screen size;
  • approximate (country/region-level) location derived from your IP address, and session information such as when and how long you play.

We use this to understand how the Game is played and to balance and improve it. We look at it in aggregated reports; it is not used to identify you. The identifiers above are pseudonymous rather than anonymous, which is why we ask first.

Advertising – Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), a mediation service that currently serves ads from Unity Ads. To show an ad, measure it, cap how often you see it and prevent fraud, these services process your IP address, device and app information, and your device's advertising ID.

  • If you agree to personalized ads, they may use this information to show ads based on your interests.
  • If you do not agree, or the age you entered is under 16, they are told so and ads are not personalized. You will still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Rewarded ads, such as an extra life or doubled coins, are always optional.

App stores and purchases

Downloads, and any in-app purchases the Game offers, are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payments are processed by them; we never see your payment details or account information.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account.
  • No precise (GPS) location.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or files.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we will not use the Game to contact you or send you marketing.

4. Your choices

  • Change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → Personalized Ads & Analytics. Turning it off stops analytics collection immediately and makes ads non-personalized.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: the Game asks for tracking permission, which you can change in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Remove everything stored on your device by uninstalling the Game.

5. Legal basis and your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar jurisdiction, analytics and personalized advertising rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalized ads are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding a free game.

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold your name or contact details, we usually cannot link analytics data to you; email us and we will help as far as we can, including by forwarding your request to the relevant provider.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game. Firebase keeps analytics data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings, at most 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain. Advertising providers retain data as described in their own policies.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. On first launch it asks your age. If the age entered is under 16, analytics stays off, consent for personalized ads is not requested, and ad providers are told to treat you as a child, so ads are not personalized. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Data sent by the Game's third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections. Since we run no servers and keep no personal data ourselves, there is no database of players that could be breached.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The latest version is always on this page, with its date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
